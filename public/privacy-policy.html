Privacy Policy

Chosen Word Bible app · Effective 16 October 2026

Chosen Word (“the app”) is made by Sam Hutson Wallace (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what information the app uses, where it goes, and the choices you have. It applies to the Android and iOS apps.

In short
  • There are no accounts, and we run no servers. Your highlights, notes, bookmarks and sermons stay on your phone. We never receive them.
  • The app shows ads from Google AdMob. Google may collect your device’s advertising ID and similar data to show and measure ads.
  • A few optional features use the internet: the online dictionary definition and the Bible maps.
  • We do not sell your information, and we have none to sell.

1. Information kept on your device

Everything you create in the app is saved only in the app’s storage on your phone:

  • highlights, verse notes, chapter and book notes, and bookmarks;
  • sermons, their earlier versions, and the sermon trash;
  • reading-plan progress, reading position, streaks and daily quiz answers;
  • settings such as theme, text size and chosen translation.

This information is never sent to us or to anyone else, and we cannot see it. It is deleted when you use Settings → Erase all my data or uninstall the app.

Backups. When you tap Back up, the app creates a file and opens your phone’s share sheet. You choose where the file goes (for example Files, Google Drive or email). The handling of that file is then governed by the service you choose. Restoring a backup only reads the file you select.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app is free and shows a banner ad, plus optional video ads that you can choose to watch for ad-free time (in preach mode, or for an hour of reading). Ads are provided by Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. To show, limit and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect:

  • your device’s advertising identifier (Android advertising ID or Apple IDFA, where allowed);
  • your IP address and the approximate location derived from it;
  • device and app information, such as model, operating system, language and app version;
  • ad interactions, such as which ads were shown or tapped.

Google uses this data as described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy. We do not receive your advertising ID or any personal information from Google. We see only overall totals, such as how many ads were shown.

Your choices

  • European Economic Area, UK and Switzerland: the app asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Ads & privacy → Privacy & ad choices.
  • iPhone and iPad: the app asks for permission before it can use your device’s advertising identifier (App Tracking Transparency). You can change this in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Android: you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone’s Settings → Privacy → Ads (the wording varies by phone).

If you decline personalised ads, you will still see ads, but they will be less tailored to you.

3. Features that use the internet

The Bible texts, the Bible dictionary and most of the app work fully offline. A few optional features connect to outside services:

  • Modern English definitions and pronunciation audio: when you look up a word while online, the app sends that word to the Free Dictionary API (dictionaryapi.dev) to fetch an everyday definition and, where available, an audio file. Only the word is sent, along with the IP address that any internet request includes.
  • Bible maps: the maps screen loads map images from OpenStreetMap. Those servers receive your IP address and the area of the map being viewed. See the OpenStreetMap Foundation privacy policy.

These services receive no notes, highlights, sermons or other content you have created.

4. Read aloud

The read-aloud feature uses your phone’s built-in text-to-speech engine (for example Google’s or Apple’s). The app passes it the Bible text being read. How that engine processes text is set by your phone’s maker, and you can choose a different engine in your phone’s settings.

5. Sharing

When you tap Share or Copy, the text you selected goes to your phone’s share sheet or clipboard, and from there to the app you pick. Nothing is shared unless you tap.

6. Permissions

The app asks only for internet access, which it uses for ads, the online dictionary and the maps. It does not access your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files, except a file you choose yourself when restoring a backup.

7. What we do not do

  • We do not ask you to create an account or give your name or email.
  • We do not run analytics or tracking of our own.
  • We do not sell, rent or trade personal information.

8. Children

Chosen Word is a general-audience app and is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact us and we will help.

9. Keeping and deleting information

Information you create stays on your device until you delete it, use Erase all my data, or uninstall the app. Sermons in the trash are deleted automatically after 30 days. We keep no copy anywhere. Data that Google collects for advertising is kept according to Google’s own policies.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the EU/UK GDPR or California law), you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal information, or to object to its use. Because we do not hold personal information about you, most requests can be met directly in the app. For advertising data, use the choices in section 2 or contact Google. You are welcome to contact us with any question or request.

11. Security

Your information is protected by your phone’s own security, such as the screen lock and app sandboxing. Connections to outside services use encrypted HTTPS. Keep backup files in a place you trust, because anyone who has a backup file can read its contents.

12. Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles information, we will update this page and the effective date above. Significant changes will also be mentioned in the app’s release notes.
